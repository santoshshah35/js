# Clean TV

Give the user clear, safe cleaning steps. Modern TV panels have delicate anti-glare
coatings that are easy to scratch or strip, so the guiding idea is: **gentlest method
first, escalate only if needed, never put liquid directly on the screen.**

## 1. Tailor if useful

If the user mentions the TV type, brand or the kind of mess (dust, fingerprints, kid
crayon, sticky residue), adapt the steps. Don't interrogate them — if nothing is
mentioned, give the general routine below. When a brand is named, suggest checking
that manufacturer's manual, since some (e.g. matte or OLED panels) advise water only.

## 2. Routine

Present these steps, adapted as needed:

1. **Power off and unplug.** Safer, and a dark, cool screen shows smudges and avoids streaks from fast evaporation.
2. **Dry dust first.** Clean, dry microfiber cloth, light pressure, wipe in one direction. Pressing hard can damage pixels.
3. **Smudges:** lightly dampen (not wet) a microfiber cloth with distilled water — tap water can leave mineral spots. Wipe, then follow with a dry cloth.
4. **Stubborn marks:** a screen-safe cleaner, or a mild 50/50 distilled water + isopropyl alcohol mix, applied to the cloth. Skip alcohol on matte/anti-glare or OLED screens unless the manufacturer allows it.
5. **Frame, stand and back:** dry or slightly damp cloth; use a soft brush or low-power vacuum brush on vents. Never spray into vents or ports.
6. **Remote (optional):** remove batteries, wipe with a cloth lightly dampened with isopropyl alcohol, use a toothpick for button gaps.
7. **Let everything dry fully** before plugging back in.

## 3. Always include the "avoid" list

Damage from the wrong product is the main real risk, so always mention:
- Paper towels, tissues, rough rags (scratch the coating)
- Window cleaner / ammonia, acetone, bleach, strong alcohol, abrasive cleaners
- Spraying anything directly on the screen (liquid seeps into the bezel)
- Pressing hard or scrubbing in circles

## 4. Special cases

- **CRT / older glass screens:** tougher glass; a damp cloth or glass-safe cleaner on the cloth is fine. Still unplug first — CRTs hold static.
- **Sticky residue:** repeated gentle passes with a damp cloth; let moisture soften it rather than scraping.
- **Scratches / dead pixels:** cleaning won't fix these; suggest the manufacturer or a repair service rather than DIY "scratch removers".

## Output format

Keep it short and scannable: a one-line intro, the numbered steps, the "avoid" list,
and any relevant special case. Offer a quick cleaning frequency tip at the end
(dust weekly-ish, wipe smudges as needed).
